# referent — codebase review (2026-08-22)

Four independent fresh-context reviews (core statistics, longitudinal statistics,
API/scope, code quality) plus `devtools::test()` (all pass) and `R CMD check`
(1 WARNING: non-ASCII in `R/plot-data.R`). Every bug below was reproduced by
running code; reproduction scripts are in the session scratchpad.

## Verdict

The design is right and the hard parts are correct: SHASH matches `mgcv::shash`
to 1e-7 (cdf, quantile, density), gaulss 1/sigma handling and the `b` offset are
correct, posterior-simulation "total" uncertainty is the right construction,
`condition_z`/`condition_norm_dist` (Schur-complement forecast) are correct and
calibrate out of sample, crossfit is cluster-stratified with in-sample
detection, support detection is sound, and CRPS is exact for Gaussian / 1e-3
for SHASH. The change-score algebra generalises PCNtoolkit's z-diff properly.

What is broken sits in the wrapper layer around that kernel: covariate
inference, site adaptation, the dynamics estimator, and model selection. All are
fixable in ~1–2 days and the fixes *remove* code. The package is also carrying
~450 lines of dead or half-implemented surface (gamlss engine, ordinal family,
parallel.R, bespoke tidyr/dplyr equivalents, 5 distribution generics) that
should be cut or replaced with ecosystem packages.

## C. Ecosystem leverage (elegant)

1. **Build `norm_dist` on `distributional`.** Verified live: SHASH as `new_dist(..., class="dist_shash")` with `cdf/quantile/density/generate` methods is ~25 lines, and `mean`, `variance`, `hilo`, printing, vctrs slicing, tibble columns and `ggdist::stat_slab(aes(xdist=))` work with zero extra code. Total-uncertainty predictive = `dist_mixture()` over coefficient draws (kills `cdf_total` and the loose-attribute hack that `recycle_pair()` copies around); the history-conditioned forecast is a ~40-line `dist_conditioned`. Removes 5 exported generics that collide by name with `distributional::cdf/variance`. `dist.R` 386 → ~180 lines. Do this now, at 0.0.0.9000.
2. **`generics`: `tidy.norm_fit`** (today's `summary`), **`glance`** (n, log score, CRPS, mean_z, var_z, coverage), **`augment.norm_fit(newdata)`** → wide z/centile matrix — the neuroimaging "give me a Z matrix" need. +40 lines.
3. **PCNtoolkit metrics**: `standardized_log_score` = −MSLL and `cor` = Rho already; add `ev`, `smse` (4 lines) and a "Coming from PCNtoolkit" article mapping Z/yhat/s2/MSLL/SMSE/EV/site-adaptation.
4. `scoringRules::crps_sample(y, generate(d, 500))` for non-Gaussian CRPS once on `distributional`.

## D. Performance (5000 × 20 outcomes, `s(age)+sex+s(site,bs="re")`)

fit const-scale 5.3 s · gaulss 11.7 s · predict conditional (100k scores) 3.1 s · total 10.6 s · calibrated 15.0 s · assess 10.2 s · gaulss fit 27.5 MB (≈1 MB/gam, 0.7 MB of it the family closure env). Hot paths: `predict.gam` called 2–3× per outcome (one `type="link", se.fit=TRUE` + `linkinv` covers all); `apply_calibration` per-row ecdf (5×); `crps_from_dist`/`variance.norm_dist` per-row `vec_slice` (do the n×K quantile matrix once). `norm_reference()` must actually strip (family env, model frame, fitted, residuals) — predict and lpmatrix still work after stripping (verified); today the "frozen" bundle is *larger* than the fit.
